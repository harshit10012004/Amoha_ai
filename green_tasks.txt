--------------------------------------------------------------
 KEY RESEARCH INSIGHTS (for feature/risk signals)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Cognitive test scores: strong NEGATIVE correlation (-0.84)
- Diabetes: most common comorbidity among dementia patients
- APOE-epsilon4 allele: significant genetic risk factor
- Family history alone: NOT definitive (48.6% vs 51.4%)
- Best-performing ML model: LDA (98% testing accuracy)
- ML models averaged 95.39% vs hybrid CNN-RNN 92.23%
  -> classical ML is preferred for MVP over deep learning
